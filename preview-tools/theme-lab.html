Theme lab (preview only)

Four ways of declaring the page's colour scheme, each drawn over the same canvas sky as the site. It shows which one Samsung Internet leaves light when the phone is in dark mode.

  1. Put the phone in dark mode (and Samsung Internet's own dark mode, if you use it).
  2. Open this page in Samsung Internet and scroll through the four tiles. Also try opening each one on its own with its link.
  3. Screenshot the tiles and send them. In a tile that works, the panel stays light with dark text.
